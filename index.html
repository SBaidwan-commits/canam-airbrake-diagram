How the air travels: compressor to brake chamber

Tractor-trailer dual air brake system, seen from above. This is the typical layout on North American trucks (Canada and US); where each valve sits changes from one make to another.

Reading the lines

    Brake chambers are shown in their working position: service brake chambers on the steer axle, spring brake chambers (service + spring) on the drive and trailer axles. The pushrod moves the slack adjuster to apply the brake. Moving dots show which way the air is going.

    Numbers to know

    • Normal operating range (MTO handbook)100–120 psi · 690–828 kPa
    • Governor cut-in below cut-out20–25 psi · 138–173 kPa
    • Safety valve opens150 psi · 1035 kPa
    • Low air-pressure warning, most trucks60 psi · 414 kPa
    • Low air-pressure warning, must activate by55 psi · 380 kPa
    • Tractor protection valve opens service lineabout 310 kPa · 45 psi
    • Trailer supply valve closes automatically20–45 psi · 138–311 kPa
